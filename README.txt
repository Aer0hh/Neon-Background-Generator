✨ Neon Background Generator ✨

HOW TO USE:
1. Open "index.html" in any modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari)
2. Customize particles, colors, and effects using the control panel
3. Click "Download PNG" to save your creation
4. Move your mouse to interact with particles

FEATURES:
• 20-500 particles with glow effects
• Real-time customization
• High-performance optimization
• Works offline (no internet required after download)

PERFECT FOR:
• Stream backgrounds
• Website headers
• Social media content
• Digital art projects

No installation required. Just open and create!
